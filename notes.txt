Turn Order
	1. Do conditional checks and increment Turn Number
		Are you dead? Should a Conditional Deck be added/ removed?
	2. Draw 3 Cards face down
		Backs of the Cards reveal what deck they are from
		Cards will be drawn from Decks with still remaining Cards
		One Card will be drawn from every Deck
		No Deck will be drawn from twice per turn unless all other Decks exhausted
		If no Cards remain exhausted Decks are shuffled
		If no Cards remain (still) as many cards as possible are Drawn
	3. Choose 1 of the 3 Cards
		Apply card Effect(s)
		Consider: Player might have a chance to use an Ability
			- Reveal Card(s)
			- Redraw Card(s)
			- Etc.
	4. Make Card Choice selection
		Apply the Choice effect(s)
		Card may:
			- Discard other Cards
			- Apply effects (Generally these will be Deck Modifications)
	5. Discard the Card
	6. Shuffle all Exhausted Decks
		If Deck is Exhausted shuffle it
		A Deck is Exhausted if all Unlocked and Drawable cards have been Discarded

Notes
	- Starting a turn with Health <= 0 is Game Over
	- To Win find a Golden Win Card
		Generally difficult to do, these Cards are often Locked in their Deck at the start
		Making correct choices on other Cards will Unlock Cards that eventually Unlock these Golden Win Cards
		These cards tend to have conditions on a choice required for winning
		If that choice is selected you win, hooray
		Need to draw a bunch of the right cards
		Card Choice will apply a Win state 
		After "winning" you get celebrated/ noticed for you achievement
		Game continues after "winning" and you can even go on to "win" again
	- Some cards provide the option to continue playing as a new character
		Things like having a child mature to a certain age let you continue as them
